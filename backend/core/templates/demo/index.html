    You should see unconfirmed suggestions with a quote from the file. Keep confirms them. A job can still use them before Keep.

  2. Open the internship Form A, page 1 — cream, serif, Imperial.

    Personal → Education → Transcript → Review. Three Continues, then Submit.

  3. Deal with this Drop both files on the panel if they are not already in the vault. Prompt: apply with my CV and transcript, stop before submit Click Deal with this. Open Details on the job card. Check the stages, the stored facts, and the transcript. Click Go.

  4. Confirm, do not submit for them When the last page is filled, the overlay asks before Submit application. Show the filled review. You may confirm or hit Don't.
